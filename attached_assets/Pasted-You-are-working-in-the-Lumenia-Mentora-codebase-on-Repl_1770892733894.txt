4) CONTENT STRUCTURE (Cambridge-style progression)

This topic focuses on recognizing and expanding special patterns:

A) Perfect Square Identities
(x + a)^2 = x^2 + 2ax + a^2
(x - a)^2 = x^2 - 2ax + a^2

B) Difference of Squares
(x + a)(x - a) = x^2 - a^2
