5) CONTENT SCOPE: FACTORISING QUADRATICS

Cover the common school progression:

A) Monic trinomials (a=1):
  x^2 + bx + c = (x+p)(x+q)

B) Difference of squares:
  x^2 - a^2 = (x-a)(x+a)

C) Non-monic trinomials (a≠1) (hard/challenge):
  ax^2 + bx + c, factor by splitting the middle term (AC method) / inspection
  e.g., 2x^2 + 7x + 3 = (2x+1)(x+3)

D) Factorising with a common factor first (hard/challenge):
  2x^2 + 4x = 2x(x+2)   (include a few so students learn “GCF first”)

Avoid:
- Completing the square (separate chapter)
- Quadratic formula (separate chapter)

6) ARCHETYPES & DIFFICULTY BANDS (Cambridge-style)

EASY (8) — monic, small integers, mostly positive c
Archetypes:
- monic_pos: x^2 + bx + c with integer factors
- monic_neg: x^2 + bx - c with opposite sign factors
Constraints:
- Choose p,q in [-9..9], p*q = c, p+q = b
- Keep |b| <= 12, |c| <= 36
Skills tags:
- ["factorise_monic", "find_factor_pairs"]

MEDIUM (8) — include difference of squares + trickier signs
Archetypes:
- diff_of_squares: x^2 - a^2
- monic_mixed: x^2 - bx + c
- gcf_then_monic: kx^2 + kbx + kc = k(x+p)(x+q) (small k)
Constraints:
- At least 2 difference_of_squares items
- Include at least 2 with negative b
Skills tags:
- ["difference_of_squares", "factorise_monic", "take_common_factor"]

HARD (7) — non-monic trinomials (a≠1) with integer factors
Archetypes:
- non_monic: ax^2 + bx + c where a in [2..6] and factorises nicely
- ac_method: requires splitting middle term (show in worked_solution)
- gcf_then_non_monic: take out common factor first, then factorise
Constraints:
- Ensure factors are integer linear factors
- Avoid huge coefficients
Skills tags:
- ["factorise_non_monic", "ac_method", "take_common_factor", "simplify_expression"]

CHALLENGE (7) — multi-step + structure recognition
Archetypes:
- non_monic_negative: ax^2 + bx - c
- rearranged: bx + x^2 + c (require reordering to standard form first)
- factorise_then_simplify: factorise and then use factors for a quick evaluation (optional if supported)
Constraints:
- Must still be autogradable
- Provide clear worked_solution steps
Skills tags:
- ["structure_recognition", "reorder_terms", "factorise_non_monic", "multi_step"]

7) WORKED SOLUTION REQUIREMENTS

For monic:
- "Find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b."
- "Those numbers are p and q."
- "So x^2 + bx + c = (x+p)(x+q)."

For difference of squares:
- "Recognise x^2 - a^2."
- "Use identity: x^2 - a^2 = (x-a)(x+a)."

For non-monic (AC method):
- "Compute a*c."
- "Find two numbers that multiply to a*c and add to b."
- "Split middle term."
- "Factor by grouping."
- "Write final factorised form."

Keep steps short but explicit.
