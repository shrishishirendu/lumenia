5) CONTENT SCOPE: GRAPHICAL SOLUTIONS OF SIMULTANEOUS EQUATIONS

Students should learn:
- Each equation represents a line.
- The solution is the intersection point (x,y).
- Interpret intersection from graph (read coordinates).
- Identify special cases:
  - One solution (lines intersect once)
  - No solution (parallel lines)
  - Infinite solutions (same line) — optional challenge

No need to teach drawing the graph from scratch (optional later); focus on reading and interpreting.

7) ARCHETYPES & DIFFICULTY BANDS (Cambridge-style)

EASY (8)

MEDIUM (8)
Archetypes:
- intersection_with_given_equations: show graph + provide equations y=mx+c for both lines
- verify_solution: graph shows intersection; ask to confirm by substitution (optional)
Constraints:
- intersection integer; slopes may be fractional but still plot cleanly
Skills tags:
- ["read_intersection", "interpret_line_equation"]

HARD (7)
Archetypes:
- fractional_intersection: intersection at simple halves (e.g., (1.5, -2)) OR keep integer but harder reading
- near_ticks: intersection not at origin and requires careful reading
- parallel_lines_no_solution: lines parallel; student answers “no solution”
Constraints:
- If using fractional intersections, keep to .5 only and clearly mark half-ticks OR avoid fractions and increase complexity another way.
- Include at least 2 “no solution” parallel cases
Answer format for no-solution:
- canonical answer string "no solution"
Skills tags:
- ["read_intersection", "parallel_lines", "no_solution_case"]

CHALLENGE (7)
Archetypes:
- same_line_infinite_solutions: two identical lines; answer “infinitely many solutions”
- choose_correct_graph: show graph and ask which pair of equations match (only if your platform supports MCQ)
- determine_number_of_solutions: based on graph (1 / 0 / infinite)
Answer format canonical:
- "infinitely many solutions"
Skills tags:
- ["classify_solutions", "coincident_lines", "parallel_lines"]
