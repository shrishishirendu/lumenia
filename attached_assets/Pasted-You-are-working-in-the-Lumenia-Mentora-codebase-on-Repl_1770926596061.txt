5) CONTENT SCOPE: FACTORISING COMMON FACTORS

This topic covers:
- Taking out a numerical common factor: 6x + 9 = 3(2x + 3)
- Taking out a variable common factor: x^2 + 5x = x(x + 5)
- Taking out a monomial common factor: 6x^2 + 9x = 3x(2x + 3)
- Common factor with negatives: -4x - 8 = -4(x + 2) (medium/hard)
- Factorising with 3 or more terms where a common factor exists: 3x + 6 + 9y = 3(x + 2 + 3y) (hard/challenge)

Avoid:
- Quadratic trinomials factoring (that’s a different chapter)
- Difference of squares factoring (different chapter)
