Students should learn:
- Rearrange one equation to make a variable the subject (usually y = ...)
- Substitute into the other equation
- Solve the resulting single-variable equation
- Substitute back to find the other variable
- Present answer as (x,y)
- Understand the link: solution = intersection point of two lines (visual support)

Include cases like:
- y expressed already: y = 2x + 3 and y = -x + 1
- x expressed already: x = 3y - 2 (medium/hard)

7) DIFFICULTY ARCHETYPES (Cambridge-style)

EASY (8)
Archetypes:
- y_equals_expression_both:
  y = mx + c and y = nx + d (substitute by equating or substituting directly)
- one_eq_y_form_other_standard:
  y = mx + c and ax + by = k (substitute y into second)
Constraints:
- m,n integers in [-3..3], avoid equal slopes
- intersection at integer point within [-5..5]
Answer format:
- "(x,y)"
Skills tags:
- ["substitution", "solve_linear", "substitute_back"]

MEDIUM (8)
Archetypes:
- x_equals_expression:
  x = py + q paired with ax + by = k (substitute x)
- substitution_requires_rearrange:
  ax + by = c and dx + ey = f (rearrange first into y=...)
- visual_substitution_link (must include metadata.visual):
  show graph + equations; solve by substitution (not by reading graph)
Constraints:
- Mostly integer solutions; allow simple halves rarely
Skills tags:
- ["rearrange", "substitution", "solve_linear", "interpret_graph"]

HARD (7)
Archetypes:
- fractional_coefficients_light:
  e.g., 2x + 3y = 12 with y = (x+1)/2
- negative_and_rearrange:
  -x + 2y = 7 and 3x + y = 5
- visual_hard (must include metadata.visual):
  show graph to confirm/check the algebra result
Constraints:
- Allow some fractional solutions (denominator <= 4)
Skills tags:
- ["substitution", "fractions", "rearrange", "verify_solution"]

CHALLENGE (7)
Archetypes:
- special_cases_parallel:
  two equations yield parallel lines => "no solution"
- special_cases_coincident:
  same line => "infinitely many solutions"
- visual_classify:
  show graph and equations; solve by substitution OR classify solution count
Constraints:
- Include at least 1 "no solution" and 1 "infinitely many solutions"
Canonical answers:
- "no solution"
- "infinitely many solutions"
Skills tags:
- ["classify_solutions", "parallel_lines", "coincident_lines", "substitution"]
