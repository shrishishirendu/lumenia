Lesson 1 content
Explanation:
"A linear equation is an equation where the variable has power 1. To solve, isolate the variable by undoing operations. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other."
Worked examples:
1) x + 5 = 12 → x = 7
2) 3x = 18 → x = 6
Guided practice (with hints):
- x − 4 = 9
- 5x = 25
- x ÷ 3 = 6

Lesson 2 content
Explanation:
"Two-step equations need two moves. Undo addition/subtraction first, then undo multiplication/division."
Worked examples:
1) 2x + 3 = 11 → x = 4
2) 5x − 7 = 18 → x = 5
Guided practice:
- 3x + 4 = 19
- 6x − 8 = 16
- 2x + 9 = 21

Lesson 3 content
Explanation:
"If brackets are present, expand first using the distributive law, then solve normally."
Worked example:
3(x + 2) = 18 → 3x + 6 = 18 → x = 4
Guided practice:
- 2(x + 5) = 16
- 4(x − 1) = 12
- −3(x + 2) = 6

Lesson 4 content
Explanation:
"Word problems describe operations on an unknown. Let the unknown be x, write an equation, solve, then check."
Worked example:
"A number increased by 7 equals 19." → x + 7 = 19 → x = 12
Guided practice:
- A number multiplied by 4 equals 28
- A number minus 5 equals 13
- Twice a number plus 6 equals 20
