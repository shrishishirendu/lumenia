PUBLISHING STRATEGY FOR LUMENIA (SAFE PUBLIC RELEASE)

Context:
- Domain lumenia.au is registered.
- Core app is still under active development.
- Auth + RBAC + student learning loop are in progress and should NOT be publicly exposed yet.
- We want public presence + credibility WITHOUT risking the app.

HIGH-LEVEL DECISION
We are publishing in TWO LAYERS:
1) Public marketing / trust layer (now)
2) Application layer (later)

This is intentional. Do not merge them yet.

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE B1 — WHAT TO PUBLISH NOW (MANDATORY)
────────────────────────────────────────

Publish ONLY a public landing page under the domain:
→ https://lumenia.au

This landing page must be:
- Accessible without login
- Safe to index
- Stable even if the app changes

DO NOT publish the app login or dashboards yet.

────────────────────────────────────────
TECHNICAL STRUCTURE (REQUIRED)
────────────────────────────────────────

1) Separate the landing page from the app
- Landing page lives at `/`
- App remains on existing Replit URL for now
- No auth middleware on landing page routes

2) Domain strategy
- lumenia.au → Landing page
- app.lumenia.au → RESERVED (do not connect yet)

3) Hosting
- Landing page can be:
  a) A static build deployed via Replit / hosting provider
  b) Or a simple server-rendered public route
- HTTPS must be enabled

────────────────────────────────────────
LANDING PAGE CONTENT (MINIMAL & FINAL)
────────────────────────────────────────

The landing page must include:

1) Hero
   - Title: “Lumenia”
   - Subtitle: “Guided learning for Years 6–12 — calm, personal, effective.”
   - Primary CTA: “Request early access”

2) What Lumenia is
   - Short explanation of guided learning
   - No heavy AI claims
   - Focus on structure, care, and progress

3) Mentora explanation
   - “Mentora is your child’s learning guide within Lumenia”
   - Avoid technical language

4) Subjects
   - Mathematics
   - English
   - “More coming soon”

5) Lead capture
   - Simple form: name, email, child year level (6–12)
   - On submit:
     - Store securely (DB if available, else server-side store)
     - OR email notification to admin
   - Show confirmation message

6) Footer
   - © Lumenia
   - Privacy
   - Terms

────────────────────────────────────────
SECURITY & SAFETY RULES (CRITICAL)
────────────────────────────────────────

- Do NOT expose:
  - /login
  - /admin
  - /student
  - /parent
  - /tutor
- Do NOT link to the app from the landing page yet
- Do NOT index app routes (robots.txt if needed)
- Do NOT move auth cookies or sessions to lumenia.au

This prevents accidental access, trust issues, and partial experiences.

────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT NOT TO DO (VERY IMPORTANT)
────────────────────────────────────────

- Do NOT point lumenia.au directly to the app
- Do NOT deploy the login page publicly yet
- Do NOT expose unfinished dashboards
- Do NOT announce “AI-powered” aggressively
- Do NOT combine marketing + app domains yet

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE B2 — LATER (NOT NOW)
────────────────────────────────────────

Only after:
- Student learning loop is stable
- Parent portal is reviewed
- Ops guardrails are hardened

Then we will:
- Deploy the app to app.lumenia.au
- Keep lumenia.au as marketing + trust layer
- Add controlled login access

────────────────────────────────────────
SUCCESS CRITERIA FOR THIS PHASE
────────────────────────────────────────

- lumenia.au loads fast and looks professional
- Parents understand what Lumenia is in <30 seconds
- Leads are captured reliably
- No accidental access to the app
- App development continues uninterrupted

This phase is about credibility, not traffic.
Stability over speed.
