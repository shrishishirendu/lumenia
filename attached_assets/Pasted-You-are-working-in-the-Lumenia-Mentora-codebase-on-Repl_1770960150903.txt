5) CONTENT SCOPE: TRIGONOMETRIC RATIOS

Focus on right-angled triangles and the definitions:

sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse  
cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse  
tan θ = opposite / adjacent  

Include:
- Finding a ratio given side lengths
- Finding missing side given angle and one side
- Finding angle given ratio (inverse trig optional medium+)
- Basic special angles (30°, 45°, 60°) in challenge

Avoid:
- Sine rule / cosine rule (separate topic)
- Radians (unless optional challenge)

7) DIFFICULTY ARCHETYPES

EASY (8)
Archetypes:
- identify_ratio: Given labeled triangle, compute sinθ, cosθ, or tanθ
- ratio_from_lengths: sinθ = ? given opposite and hypotenuse
Constraints:
- Use Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13)
- Answers as simplified fractions
Skills:
- ["identify_opposite_adjacent", "basic_ratio"]

MEDIUM (8)
Archetypes:
- find_missing_side: Given θ and one side, find another side
- find_ratio_decimal: Round to 2 decimal places (if allowed)
- inverse_ratio_simple: Given sinθ = 0.5, find θ (30°, 45°, 60° only)
Constraints:
- Keep angles to common special or easy calculator values
Skills:
- ["apply_sin_cos_tan", "solve_for_side"]

HARD (7)
Archetypes:
- algebraic_side_expression: Opposite = 2x, adjacent = 3x, find tanθ
- two_step: Use Pythagoras first, then trig ratio
- coordinate_triangle: Triangle plotted on coordinate grid
Constraints:
- Keep arithmetic manageable
Skills:
- ["pythagoras", "multi_step_trig"]

CHALLENGE (7)
Archetypes:
- special_angles_exact: sin30°, cos45°, tan60°
- prove_identity_numeric: Verify sin^2θ + cos^2θ = 1 for given triangle
- mixed_visual_reasoning: Given diagram missing label, deduce needed ratio
Constraints:
- Provide exact answers using fractions or sqrt forms where appropriate
Skills:
- ["exact_values", "trig_identity"]
